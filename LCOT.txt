Missions that yield Tyleon (Lost City of Tyleon, LCOT) plans
============================================================

Source: ../lacuna_server/mission/missions/*.mission
Building names: "Lost City of Tyleon (A)" .. "(I)"
               (Lacuna::DB::Result::Building::LCOTa .. LCOTi)

25 mission files each award exactly one Tyleon plan (level 1, qty 1,
extra_build_level 0) and nothing else; none have "requires" fields.

Shorthands
----------
G20     = 1 each of all 20 glyphs (anthracite, bauxite, beryl, chalcopyrite,
          chromite, fluorite, galena, goethite, gold, gypsum, halite,
          kerogen, magnetite, methane, monazite, rutile, sulfur, trona,
          uraninite, zircon)
ORES5k  = 5000 each of those same 20 ores
"uni"   = max_university_level (highest university level at which the
          mission is still offered)

Plan A (unique)
----------------
The Mortal Coil (jandor-trading-the-mortal-coil.mission, uni 30)
  10 essentia, 50M happiness, G20, 2x supply_pod4 -> inhabited isolationist
  planets (size 30-75, outside zone), 1 each: bleeder, observatory_seeker,
  placebo5, scow, security_ministry_seeker, snark3, spaceport_seeker,
  sweeper.

----------------
Strange Delivery (norway_strange_delivery.mission, uni 30)

  23 permanent plans (AlgaePond, AmalgusMeadow, BeeldebanNest,
  CitadelOfKnope, CrashedShipSite, DentonBrambles, GasGiantPlatform,
  GeoThermalVent, Grove, HallsOfVrbansk x2, InterDimensionalRift,
  KalavianRuins, Lagoon, LapisForest, LibraryOfJith, MalcudField,
  NaturalSpring, OracleOfAnid, PantheonOfHagness, Ravine,
  TempleOfTheDrajilites, TerraformingPlatform, Volcano).

Summary
-------

3-4 different missions.
